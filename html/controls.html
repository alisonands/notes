Root Locus

  • Steps to sketching and determining parameters of the Root Locus plots are as follows
    1. Poles and Zeros
      • Define the transfer function ex. $$G(s) = \frac{2s + 10}{s(s + 1)(s + 3)}$$
      • The poles are -3, -1 and the zeros are -5
      • The number of poles are 3 and the number of zeros are 1
    2. Identify number of branches
      • num of branches = num of poles
    3. Breakaway/in points
      • $$\frac{dK}{ds} = 0$$
        • where $$K = -\frac{1}{G(s)H(s)}$$
    4. Asymptotes
    5. Crossover points
    6. Angle of arrival and departure
